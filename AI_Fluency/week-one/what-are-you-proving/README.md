# What Are You Proving?

Portfolio: https://austinpavia.dev

## Proof Statement

My portfolio exists to prove that I can build clean, reliable backend APIs for real product workflows. The specific person I want to convince is an engineering hiring manager or technical founder looking for a junior backend-focused full-stack developer who can turn requirements into working systems. The one action I want them to take is to contact me after reviewing my GitHub projects and seeing that my work is organized, tested through real usage, and documented clearly enough for another developer to run.

## Why This Needs To Exist

A CV or LinkedIn profile can list my tools, but my portfolio proves the stronger thing: I can ship working backend projects with clear docs, real endpoints, and enough UI awareness to make the system understandable.

## AI Thinking Partner Notes

I used Codex to pressure-test the statement instead of writing it as a generic bio. The first version was too broad because it tried to prove backend engineering, full-stack development, and UI/UX equally. I narrowed it to one primary claim: reliable backend APIs for real product workflows. I kept UI/UX as supporting context, not the main proof.

Key questions I answered:

- What is the single skill the portfolio should prove first?
- Who exactly needs to believe it?
- What action should that person take?
- What can this portfolio prove that LinkedIn cannot?

The change I made was removing broad claims like "creative full-stack developer" and replacing them with a clearer proof target: backend APIs, product workflows, GitHub evidence, and readable documentation.
