## Image Strategy

The portfolio should use real captures for work, a real photo for me, and only quiet generated or designed visuals for connective tissue. The design should frame the projects, not become the most memorable part of the page.

## Real Capture Over AI Decisions

I chose a real portrait for the identity section because a portfolio should not use a generated image to represent the person behind the work.

I chose real screenshots or real project assets for APoS, EXPY, LUMEN, and CodeGuard AI / Compass AI because the viewer needs proof of shipped systems. AI-generated interface images would look cleaner in the short term, but they would not prove that the projects exist or work.

## AI / Generated Connective Tissue

The only non-work visual I would keep is the simple favicon/monogram style from the identity kit. If I generate connective imagery later, it should follow this prompt and stay visually quiet:

```text
Create a minimal portfolio texture for a backend-focused full-stack developer. Use a near-black background, near-white thin lines, and one restrained blue accent. The mood should be calm, technical, and quiet. Avoid glowing blobs, fake glass, abstract AI faces, 3D cubes, or anything that looks like a startup hero graphic.
```

## Rejected Generated Image

Rejected idea: an abstract glowing "AI network" hero image with neon nodes and glass shapes.

Why I rejected it: it would make the portfolio look like a generic AI landing page. It does not show my backend work, my APIs, my data models, or my actual products. A clean claim plus real project screenshots is stronger.
